## What you will learn

- The four SORA 2.5 ground risk mitigations: M1(A), M1(B), M1(C) and M2
- How robustness levels translate into GRC credits
- How to write a mitigation claim that an assessor can accept

## The mitigations and their credits

| Mitigation | Low | Medium | High |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **M1(A)** Sheltering | -1 | -2 | not available |
| **M1(B)** Operational restrictions | not available | -1 | -2 |
| **M1(C)** Ground observation | -1 | not available | not available |
| **M2** Effects of UA impact dynamics reduced | not available | -1 | -2 |

- **M1(A) Sheltering**: people below are protected by buildings or vehicles, so a UA that cannot penetrate a normal dwelling reduces exposure.
- **M1(B) Operational restrictions**: you reduce the number of people at risk by choosing time of day, route, altitude or areas to avoid.
- **M1(C) Ground observation**: a tactical mitigation, where observers on the ground can see the area below and stop the operation if people enter.
- **M2 Impact dynamics reduced**: for example a parachute, a frangible design or an energy-absorbing structure. Parachutes for small UAS can be tested against standards such as ASTM F3322.

## Final GRC

The final GRC is the iGRC plus the credits you can justify, and it can never be lower than **1**. SORA also limits how far mitigations may reduce the result, so read the current text of Step 3 for the cap that applies.

## Writing a claim the assessor can accept

Every credit needs three things:

1. **Claim**: "M2 at medium robustness, a parachute reduces the effect of impact."
2. **Integrity argument**: what the mitigation does, with test data, standards or a manufacturer declaration
3. **Assurance**: how you know it works, for example flight tests, third-party validation or an inspection program

<div class="callout"><strong>Worked example, continued.</strong> iGRC 4. You add a certified parachute at medium robustness for M2 (-1) and an operational restriction that avoids the only village on the route for M1(B) at medium robustness (-1). Final GRC = 4 - 1 - 1 = <strong>2</strong>.</div>
