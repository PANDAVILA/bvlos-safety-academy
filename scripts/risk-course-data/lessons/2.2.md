## What you will learn

- How to read the SORA 2.5 intrinsic Ground Risk Class (iGRC) table
- How to measure population density for your volume
- When a controlled ground area or a very light UA changes the answer

## The two inputs

1. **UA size and speed class**, using the maximum characteristic dimension (for a fixed wing, the wingspan) and the maximum speed: 1 m / 25 m/s, 3 m / 35 m/s, 8 m / 75 m/s, 20 m / 120 m/s or 40 m / 200 m/s. Use the first class whose dimension **and** speed you do not exceed.
2. **Population density** of the area under the operation, in people per km².

## The iGRC table (SORA 2.5)

| Population density | 1 m / 25 m/s | 3 m / 35 m/s | 8 m / 75 m/s | 20 m / 120 m/s | 40 m / 200 m/s |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Controlled ground area | 1 | 1 | 2 | 3 | 3 |
| < 5 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 | 6 |
| < 50 | 3 | 4 | 5 | 6 | 7 |
| < 500 | 4 | 5 | 6 | 7 | 8 |
| < 5,000 | 5 | 6 | 7 | 8 | 9 |
| < 50,000 | 6 | 7 | 8 | 9 | 10 |
| > 50,000 | 7 | 8 | not part of SORA | not part of SORA | not part of SORA |

Population density is in people per km². A **final** GRC above 7 puts the operation outside the specific category, so a high iGRC must be brought down by mitigations or the operation redesigned.

## Special cases

- **Controlled ground area**: the operator controls who is below. If you can demonstrate it, you use the first row.
- **Very light UA**: a UA of 250 g or less with a maximum speed of 25 m/s or less is iGRC 1 regardless of density, unless it flies over assemblies of people.
- **Worst case wins**: use the highest density that applies to the volume and the adjacent area you are assessing, not the average over a large region.

## Where to get density data

National statistics offices, the European GHSL population grid and census-based layers are common sources. Record the source, the date and the resolution in your dossier. Local events can change density: a stadium next to a rural route changes the answer.

<div class="callout"><strong>Worked example.</strong> A fixed wing with a 2.8 m wingspan and 30 m/s maximum speed uses the 3 m / 35 m/s class. Over a rural area below 50 people per km², the iGRC is <strong>4</strong>.</div>
