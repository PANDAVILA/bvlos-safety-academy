## Build your own safety case

Use the downloadable templates to apply what you learned to a real operation of your own.

## Templates in this lesson

- **BVLOS Safety Case template (Word)**: document control, operation description, ground risk, air risk, SAIL, containment, OSO matrix, FAA hazard table, traceability and sign-off
- **SORA 2.5 Workbook (Excel)**: iGRC lookup, ground mitigations and final GRC, initial and residual ARC with TMPR, SAIL table, an OSO tracker and an FAA-style hazard log with automatic risk levels

## Exercise

1. Pick one real operation. Write the Step 1 description and draw the operational volume with the ground risk buffer and the adjacent area.
2. In the workbook, enter the UA class and population density and read the iGRC. Add each ground mitigation with a written justification.
3. Choose the initial ARC from your airspace data. Add strategic mitigations and note the TMPR.
4. Read the SAIL. Try one design change and record how it changes the SAIL and the evidence burden.
5. Fill in the OSO tracker with your evidence and the document reference for each row.
6. Complete the FAA hazard log for the same operation with severity and likelihood before and after mitigations.
