## Welcome

This course teaches you to assess the risk of a BVLOS operation and to turn that assessment into a **safety case** that an authority, a client or an insurer can read and accept. You will work through ground risk, air risk, SAIL, containment and Operational Safety Objectives using the JARUS **SORA 2.5** method, then build the evidence package for an **FAA Part 107 waiver** and for an **EASA specific category authorization**.

It is written for Operations Managers, Safety Managers, Chief Remote Pilots, compliance leads and consultants who prepare operational dossiers.

## How the course works

- **Modules 1 to 5**: short video lessons, each with a diagram, written notes and the full narration transcript. Every module ends with a 5-question check.
- **Module 6**: a capstone toolkit with an editable Safety Case template and a SORA workbook in Excel, then a 50-question final assessment. The pass mark is 80%.

## What the course is mapped to

"Mapped to" means the topics follow these documents. It does not mean the course is endorsed or approved by any authority.

- **JARUS SORA 2.5** (Specific Operations Risk Assessment) and its annexes
- **Regulation (EU) 2019/947**, Article 11, and the EASA predefined risk assessments (PDRA)
- **14 CFR Part 107** waivers, with the FAA's waiver evaluation guidance, and the **proposed Part 108** rule

<div class="callout"><strong>Two systems, one way of thinking.</strong> The FAA does not use SORA. EASA authorities do. Both ask the same questions: what can go wrong on the ground, what can go wrong in the air, how do you control it, and how do you prove it? This course teaches the method once and shows how it feeds both pathways.</div>

## What you will be able to do

- Explain what a safety case is and how it differs from a risk table
- Choose the right regulatory pathway for a BVLOS operation in the US or the EU
- Define the operational volume and calculate the intrinsic and final Ground Risk Class
- Determine the Air Risk Class and the tactical mitigation performance requirement
- Derive the SAIL, containment level and Operational Safety Objectives
- Assemble a waiver or authorization package with traceable evidence
