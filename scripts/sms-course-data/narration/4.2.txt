One of the highest-risk moments in any UAS company is rapid growth or technical change. A new autopilot firmware release, a move from daytime VLOS to nighttime BVLOS, or onboarding third-party maintenance contractors can silently invalidate your existing risk controls. That is why Safety Assurance includes a formal Management of Change workflow. Before any significant technical or operational change goes live, Management of Change triggers a targeted SRM review to catch newly introduced hazards.

When an incident or near miss does occur, Safety Assurance conducts a Root Cause Analysis. We never stop at pilot error. Pilot error is a symptom, not a root cause. Using structured tools like the 5 Whys and HFACS, the Human Factors Analysis and Classification System, we trace backward from the event to uncover flawed training, ambiguous checklists, or organizational scheduling pressure.

Then we implement permanent corrective actions, and verify that they worked through scheduled audits under your Internal Evaluation Program.
