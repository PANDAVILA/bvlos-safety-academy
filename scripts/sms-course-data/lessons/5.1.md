## What you will learn

- Role-based training for executives, safety staff and crews
- How closed-loop communication keeps reporting alive
- How a Just Culture separates error, at-risk behavior and recklessness

## Training and communication

- **Executives**: resources and accountability
- **Safety Managers**: SRM, investigation, data analysis
- **RPICs and crew**: abnormal procedures, CRM, human factors
- **Recurrent training** using lessons from your own events and industry occurrences
- **Safety bulletins and alerts** for urgent issues, with read-and-sign tracking
- **Closed loop**: always tell the reporter what you did about their report

## Just Culture

| Behavior | Example | Response |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Human error | Misreading a cell voltage in a busy turnaround | Console, fix the system, share the lesson |
| At-risk behavior | Skipping a compass check to save two minutes | Coach, remove incentives for shortcuts |
| Reckless behavior | Flying under the influence, deliberately entering a restricted TFR | Disciplinary action |

Just Culture is not blame-free. It is fair: honest mistakes are treated as system problems, and conscious disregard of risk is held accountable.
