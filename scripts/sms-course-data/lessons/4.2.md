## What you will learn

- When and how to run Management of Change
- How to investigate with the 5 Whys and HFACS
- How audits verify that corrective actions worked

## Management of Change (MOC)

Change is the main source of new hazards in a growing drone company. Trigger an SRM review whenever you change:

- Firmware or GCS software
- Operating rule or mode: VLOS to BVLOS, day to night
- Geography or terrain
- Organization: rapid growth, new contractors, outsourced maintenance

## Root cause analysis

- **5 Whys**: keep asking why until you reach a cause you can fix in the system
- **HFACS**: classify contributing factors across organizational influences, supervision, preconditions and unsafe acts

"Pilot error" is a symptom. Look for flawed training, ambiguous checklists or scheduling pressure behind it.

## Internal Evaluation Program (IEP)

Scheduled and unannounced audits of flight logs, maintenance and battery records and crew currency. After a corrective action, schedule a follow-up check to confirm it worked.
