## Build your own SMS

Use the downloadable templates to apply what you learned to your own operation.

## Templates in this lesson

- **UAS SMS Manual (Word)**: editable manual with policy statement, accountabilities, ERP quick-action table, 5x5 matrix definitions, sample hazard register and a confidential hazard report form
- **Risk Register (Excel)**: 5x5 matrix with automatic risk zone, a hazard log and three pre-filled example hazards

## Exercise

1. Replace the placeholders in the manual with your company details and have your Accountable Executive review the policy statement.
2. Choose one real operation and complete steps 1 to 5 of the SRM process in the Risk Register. Include at least three hazards and the residual risk after controls.
3. Check any item marked "example" against your own aircraft, procedures and regulations. Likelihood thresholds and distances in the templates are teaching examples, not requirements.
4. Plan your first ERP tabletop exercise and schedule it.
