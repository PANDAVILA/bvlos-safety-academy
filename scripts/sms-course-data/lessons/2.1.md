## What you will learn

- Why management commitment decides whether an SMS works
- What a Safety Policy Statement must contain
- Who the Accountable Executive is and what cannot be delegated

## The Safety Policy Statement

A written policy, signed by the Accountable Executive, communicated to everyone and reviewed regularly. It should commit to:

- Providing the human and financial resources the SMS needs
- Non-punitive reporting under a Just Culture
- Clear standards for acceptable and unacceptable behavior

## The Accountable Executive

Under 14 CFR §5.25 the Accountable Executive has final authority over operations, controls the financial and human resources and carries ultimate responsibility for safety performance. In a UAS company this is usually the CEO, President or Managing Director.

The AE can delegate daily administration. The AE cannot delegate accountability. If a mitigation needs redundant C2 links or extra Visual Observers, the AE decides and funds it.
