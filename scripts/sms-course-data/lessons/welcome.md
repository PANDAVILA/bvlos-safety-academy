## Welcome

This course teaches you to design, run and improve a **Safety Management System (SMS)** for an unmanned aircraft operation. It is organized around the four pillars used by ICAO and the FAA: Safety Policy, Safety Risk Management, Safety Assurance and Safety Promotion.

It is written for Accountable Executives, Chief Pilots, UAS Safety Managers, Remote Pilots in Command and drone delivery operators.

## How the course works

- **Modules 1 to 5**: short video lessons, each with a diagram, written notes and the full narration transcript. Every module ends with a 5-question check.
- **Module 6**: a capstone toolkit with an editable SMS Manual and a Risk Register, then a 50-question final assessment. The pass mark is 80%.

## What the course is mapped to

The syllabus is mapped to the following references. "Mapped to" means the topics follow these documents. It does not mean the course is endorsed or approved by any authority.

- **FAA 14 CFR Part 5** (Safety Management Systems) and **AC 120-92D**
- **ICAO Annex 19** and **Doc 9859**, the Safety Management Manual
- **14 CFR Part 107**, **Part 135** and **Part 89**, where they connect to SMS practice

<div class="callout"><strong>Who needs an SMS?</strong> Part 5 requires an SMS for Part 121 air carriers, Part 135 certificate holders, §91.147 air tour operators and certain Part 21 certificate holders. Part 107 operators are not required to have one, but a voluntary SMS strengthens waiver applications and client safety cases.</div>

## What you will be able to do

- Explain how safety thinking evolved and use the Swiss Cheese model to analyze an accident
- Map the regulatory architecture from ICAO Annex 19 to FAA Part 5
- Write a Safety Policy and define accountabilities and an Emergency Response Plan
- Run the five-step SRM process and use the 5x5 risk matrix and Hierarchy of Controls
- Set up performance indicators, hazard reporting, Management of Change and root cause analysis
- Build a Just Culture and a closed-loop communication system
