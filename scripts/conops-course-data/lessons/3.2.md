## What you will learn

- The difference between cooperative and non-cooperative traffic
- The main detect-and-avoid options and what each can and cannot see
- How to define a detect-and-avoid concept and validate it

## Cooperative and non-cooperative traffic

- **Cooperative**: aircraft that broadcast their position, for example with ADS-B or a transponder
- **Non-cooperative**: aircraft that do not, such as many light aircraft, gliders, paragliders, drones and some helicopters

Your concept must handle both. Many mid-air encounters at low altitude involve aircraft with no transponder.

## Detect-and-avoid options

| Option | Strength | Limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Airspace observers or visual observers | Simple, flexible | Range, terrain, visibility, fatigue |
| ADS-B In or transponder receivers | Reliable for equipped traffic | Does not see non-cooperative aircraft |
| Ground-based radar | Sees non-cooperative traffic in its coverage | Coverage, clutter, licensing |
| Onboard radar, optical, infrared or acoustic sensors | Moves with the UA | Range, weather, false alarms, integration |
| Segregated airspace | Removes most traffic | Needs authorization and coordination |

## Define the concept

1. **What is detected**: types of traffic and range
2. **How the crew is alerted** and how fast
3. **Decision rule**: who decides, and what the avoidance manoeuvre is
4. **Well clear**: the separation you protect
5. **Time budget**: detection, decision, command, manoeuvre

## Validate it

Standards such as **ASTM F3442** and **RTCA DO-365** give performance requirements for detect-and-avoid systems. In FAA waiver guidance, the expected validation grows with risk: an applicant statement and data for lower risk, third-party validation with a standard for medium risk, and certification for high risk. Always evaluate cooperative and non-cooperative traffic separately.

## Observers: a worked calculation

If detect-and-avoid takes **30 seconds** and the UA flies at **120 knots**, it covers 1 NM. With a detection distance of 1.5 NM, the maximum distance of the UA from the observer is about 0.5 NM.

<div class="callout"><strong>Remember:</strong> a detect-and-avoid concept is a chain: sensor, alert, decision, command, manoeuvre. Test the whole chain, not just the sensor.</div>
