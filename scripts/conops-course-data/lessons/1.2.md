## What you will learn

- How to write a mission statement with measurable limits
- How to describe the operating environment so risks can be derived from it
- How to record assumptions and limitations

## Mission description

State the mission in one sentence, then list the facts that bound it:

- **Purpose**: inspect a 42 km gas pipeline, deliver medical samples between two hospitals, survey a wind farm
- **Frequency and duration**: flights per day, hours per flight, season, day or night
- **Payload and tasks**: sensors, release mechanisms, data products
- **Success criteria**: what the client receives and when

## Typical BVLOS use cases

- **Linear infrastructure** such as pipelines, power lines and railways: long corridors, rural, low population, repetitive routes
- **Wind and solar farms**: wide areas with obstacles, access roads, local airspace
- **Agriculture and environment**: large areas, low altitude, seasonal weather
- **Logistics and delivery**: point-to-point routes, mixed populations, takeoff and landing infrastructure
- **Public safety and emergency**: variable locations, short notice, coordination with other agencies

## Operating environment

Describe every factor that can change the safety case or the performance:

- **Terrain and obstacles**: elevation range, masts, lines, wind turbines
- **Population**: residential areas, roads, railways, events and the highest density near the route
- **Airspace**: class, restricted and danger areas, nearby airfields, helicopter routes
- **Weather**: wind, temperature, icing, visibility and the limits for the aircraft
- **Radio environment**: cellular coverage, interference, spectrum constraints
- **Infrastructure**: launch and landing sites, emergency landing areas, access for the crew

## Assumptions and limitations

Write down what must be true for the operation to be safe, such as "ground crew at the launch site", "C2 coverage above X dBm along the route" or "wind below 12 m/s". These become the **limits** in the Operations Manual and the go/no-go criteria on the day.

<div class="callout"><strong>Example.</strong> "Daylight BVLOS inspection of a 42 km pipeline corridor, flown at 80 to 120 m AGL, in uncontrolled airspace, in rural areas below 50 people per km², with a fixed-wing UA with a 2.8 m wingspan."</div>
