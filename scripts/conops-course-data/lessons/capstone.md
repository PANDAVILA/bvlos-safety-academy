## Build your own ConOps and Operations Manual

Use the downloadable templates to apply what you learned to a real operation.

## Templates in this lesson

- **BVLOS ConOps template (Word)**: the twelve-section structure with prompts, tables for volumes, routes, airspace and assumptions
- **BVLOS Operations Manual template (Word)**: the twelve-chapter structure with example procedures, a go/no-go table and an occurrence flow
- **Operations Toolkit (Excel)**: operational volume and corridor calculator, link budget, go/no-go sheet and a crew competence matrix with expiry warnings

## Exercise

1. Pick one real mission. Write the mission statement and the environment section of the ConOps.
2. In the toolkit, enter the aircraft speed, reaction time, navigation error and height. Read the FG, CV, GRB and the corridor footprint.
3. Mark the launch, landing and emergency landing areas and check the spacing against the glide range.
4. Build the link budget for the worst point of the route and write the margin you require.
5. List the crew roles and their required competences in the matrix.
6. Adapt the manual's go/no-go table to your own aircraft limits. Replace every example number.
