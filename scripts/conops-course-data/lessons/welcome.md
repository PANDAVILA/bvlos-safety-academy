## Welcome

This course teaches you to design a BVLOS operation and to write the two documents that carry it: the **Concept of Operations (ConOps)** and the **Operations Manual**. You will define the mission, size the operational volume, plan routes and airspace coordination, specify the C2 link and detect-and-avoid concept, assign crew roles, write normal and emergency procedures, and assemble a manual that matches what you promised the authority.

It is written for Operations Managers, Chief Remote Pilots, mission planners, engineers and consultants who prepare BVLOS dossiers for linear infrastructure, energy, agriculture, public safety and logistics.

## How the course works

- **Modules 1 to 5**: short video lessons, each with a diagram, written notes and the full narration transcript. Every module ends with a 5-question check.
- **Module 6**: a capstone toolkit with an editable ConOps template, an Operations Manual template and an Excel toolkit, then a 50-question final assessment. The pass mark is 80%.

## What the course is mapped to

"Mapped to" means the topics follow these documents. It does not mean the course is endorsed or approved by any authority.

- **JARUS SORA 2.5** terms for the operational volume (flight geography, contingency volume, ground risk buffer, adjacent area)
- **Regulation (EU) 2019/947** and the content of an Operations Manual for the specific category
- **FAA 14 CFR Part 107** waiver guidance for a Concept of Operations, and the proposed Part 108 roles

## What you will be able to do

- Write a ConOps that answers the questions an authority, an insurer and a client ask
- Size the flight geography, contingency volume and ground risk buffer
- Design routes, corridors, launch sites and emergency landing areas
- Plan airspace integration and coordination
- Specify the C2 link budget and a detect-and-avoid concept
- Define crew roles and competence, and write normal and emergency procedures
- Assemble and maintain an Operations Manual that matches your risk assessment
